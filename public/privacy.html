aim2balance.ai — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-09-19 · Version 1.0

Introduction

aim2balance.ai connects AI usage to verified ecological restoration. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how we store it, and which choices you have. We aim for minimal data collection and strong user control.

Summary (short)

  • We do not collect personal data unless you explicitly opt in and register.
  • All non‑shared data kept for normal local operation is stored in your browser's localStorage.
  • When you opt in and register, we collect basic account information and usage data (requests and service tokens). We do not read or store your prompts or private message content.

Controller & Contact

Data controller: aim2balance.ai (pilot phase with Bergwaldprojekt e.V.)

Contact: Kais Radwan, privacy@aim2balance.ai

What we collect

Local-only data

By default the extension and our websites store runtime state, preferences and non-sensitive operational data in the browser localStorage. This data stays on your device and is not automatically transmitted to our servers.

Data collected after opt‑in / registration

If you choose to create an account and opt in to cloud features, we will collect the minimum necessary information to operate the service. This typically includes:

  • Account email and display name
  • Authentication tokens (securely stored for your session)
  • Usage metadata: timestamps of requests, request types, aggregate token counts or token‑equivalents used for our footprint calculator

We explicitly do not collect or store the text of your prompts, files you provide, or other private content — we never inspect or log prompt contents.

Why we collect this data

  • Authentication & account management (only after you opt in).
  • To estimate resource usage and translate it into our m² restoration metric.
  • To provide a user dashboard showing your impact and exportable reports.

Legal basis

For registered users, our legal bases include contract performance (providing the service you requested) and your explicit consent for optional features. For analytics or other non-essential processing we rely on your consent.

How we store and protect data

  • LocalStorage data: remains on your device unless you choose to sync or export it.
  • Server data (registered users): stored on EU/EEA servers per our hosting choice. We employ industry-standard protections including TLS in transit, encrypted disks at rest, access controls, and regular backups.
  • Access to personal data is strictly limited to authorised personnel and to the extent required to operate the service.

Backups & retention

Registered user data is backed up daily and retained according to our retention policy. If you request deletion of your account, we will remove your personal data from live systems and schedule deletion from backups in line with our data retention schedule.

Third parties & subprocessors

We may use third-party services (hosting providers, payment processors) to operate the service. These subprocessors operate under a signed Data Processing Agreement (DPA). Current providers are listed in our public DPA appendix on the website.

Cookies & tracking

We do not enable tracking or analytics by default. If you explicitly enable optional analytics in your account settings, we will collect aggregated, non-identifying usage metrics to improve the product. You can disable this at any time in your account settings.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. To exercise any of these rights contact us at privacy@aim2balance.ai.

How to delete your data

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Go to Account → Privacy → Delete account.
  3. Confirm via email. After confirmation we will remove personal data from live databases and schedule backup purges according to our retention policy.

Security incidents

In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting personal data, we will notify affected users and the relevant supervisory authority in accordance with applicable law.

Children

Our services are not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us for removal.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will publish the effective date at the top and notify registered users by email for material changes.

For questions or to exercise your data rights contact: privacy@aim2balance.ai
